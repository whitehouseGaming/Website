A bring-your-own-Firebase HR and project management tool aimed at startups that want core HRMS functionality — attendance, project tracking, team management — without vendor lock-in on the backend.

## Tech Notes

Startups connect their own Firebase project; the app layer ships as a configurable client on top of it, keeping data ownership with the customer.
