A consumer-facing VR driving school experience for Meta Quest, aimed at giving learner drivers a low-stakes way to build confidence behind the wheel before getting on real roads.

## Tech Notes

Built on Unity with the XR Interaction Toolkit and OpenXR, targeting standalone Meta Quest hardware.
